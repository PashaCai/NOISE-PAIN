ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

See the sound.
Feel a little closer.

This work invites people without noise sensitivity to consider how overwhelming everyday sounds can feel. Frequency, energy and sudden changes become pressure, overlapping contours and sharp textures: a way to approach sounds that others cannot simply tune out.

01 Low frequenciesBroad swells, compression and slow waves.
02 Mid frequenciesFolding contours and irregular intersections.
03 High frequenciesFine spikes, grains and tightly drawn textures.
04 TransientsBrief expansions that follow sudden changes in sound.

Drag to explore the form. Scroll or use + / − to zoom; double-click to reset. Space plays or pauses; Esc pauses audio. Selecting a recording starts it from the beginning. Muting preserves the moving image.

The visuals use the actual spectra of these ten recordings. Their forms are an artistic interpretation, not a clinical measure of anyone’s distress. Band percentages show relative energy within a recording, not environmental sound pressure. Recordings are numbered because the original files did not include scene names.
